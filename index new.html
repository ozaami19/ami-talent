Who we are

A place where talent belongs.

AMI TALENT is a community initiative based in Kigali, Rwanda. It aims to create space for people to present their abilities, celebrate progress and connect with people who believe in their potential.

🌱

Our mission

To encourage people to recognise their abilities, showcase their work and access useful guidance and connections.

🌍

Our vision

A community where talent is valued, opportunities are shared and people are encouraged to keep improving.

Many paths, one community
